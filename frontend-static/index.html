How the path adapts

  1. Watch

    Lectures play inside the lesson page. Your position and real watch time are saved as you go.

  2. Check

    A short quiz after each lecture updates your mastery for every topic it covers.

  3. Adjust

    Your weekly plan sends you back to weak topics, to practice for close ones, and forward when you are ready.
